Working with your flow

An arrow means the step at its end depends on the step at its start.

Stages arrange steps by their dependencies, from left to right. A step only needs its own prerequisites to finish; unrelated steps in earlier stages do not hold it up. Selecting a step highlights its upstream and downstream paths.

Choose Freeform to use your saved positions. Dragging a card or moving it with arrow keys switches to Freeform from the current stage arrangement. Undo restores the previous layout. Switching views alone does not change your project.

Edit list numbering in Details → Hierarchy. Child numbers follow their parent automatically; assigning an occupied main-step number swaps the two numbers.

Smaller child cards are parts of a larger step. Add one with the small + on a card or Details → Add child. Dotted branches mean “part of”; arrows mean “depends on.” Parentage never creates a dependency or changes status automatically.

A new dependency between a child and an ungrouped step can put that step in the same parent branch. Existing branches are kept. Undo reverses both the link and the assignment.

The minimap shows the whole project, including steps outside Focus or inside collapsed branches. Drag anywhere in the minimap to pan the canvas. Click a miniature step to navigate; Enter on the map shows the full workflow.

Dragging a parent moves its descendants. Collapse a branch using its children badge. Click a hidden-link label to reveal the real dependencies. Deleting a parent promotes its children without deleting their work.

Project titles request your local Avenir Next Condensed Ultra Light with a synthetic bold treatment. A condensed fallback is used when unavailable. No fonts are bundled.
